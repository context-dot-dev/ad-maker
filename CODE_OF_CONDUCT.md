# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for your impact.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and intellectual-property rights.
- Help make the project accessible to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and a fair opportunity to respond.

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@context.dev](mailto:hello@context.dev) with `Branda conduct` in the subject. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Do not use public issues to report sensitive conduct concerns.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
